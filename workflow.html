PRODUCT WORKFLOW · OWNER-LIGHT

People make the deal.
LOOP makes the connection.

LOOP is a classifieds marketplace. Sellers publish their own genuine item photos and details. Buyers contact sellers directly by chat or call. They agree on price, inspection, payment and handover themselves.

Core buyer–seller journey

📷

1. Seller posts an ad

  • Uploads genuine photos of the actual item
  • Adds title, category, condition, price and location
  • Chooses free or promoted listing
  • May optionally enable OpenBid
→
🗂️

2. Listing is discoverable

  • Appears in search and category pages
  • Locality and filters help discovery
  • Promoted ads are clearly labelled
  • Report listing option is available
🔎

3. Buyer explores

  • Reviews real photos and item details
  • Checks price and condition
  • Contacts seller by in-app chat or call
→
💬

4. They negotiate directly

  • Ask questions and make an offer
  • Agree on meeting and item inspection
  • Decide whether to proceed
🤝

5. Buyer and seller complete the deal

They mutually decide the final price, payment method, pickup or delivery, and whether to close the deal. LOOP does not take custody of the item or participate in routine transactions.

What the owner does—and doesn't do

⚙️

Platform operations

Manage categories, listing rules, paid promotions, ad placements, user reports, spam and restricted content.

📈

Business growth

Review listing views, enquiries, paid promotion revenue, ad revenue, seller retention and category performance.

🧍

Not a transaction middleman

No routine price negotiation, collecting money, holding stock, arranging every delivery, or approving each normal deal.

Revenue model

Free listing

Basic ads help build supply and attract users.

Paid promotion

Seller pays to feature or boost an ad for a defined period.

Advertising

Clearly labelled display or sponsored placements can earn ad revenue.

OpenBid is an optional listing type, not the default experience. Real payments for promotions and ads require a backend and payment/ad provider integration.

Safety guardrails

  • Use genuine item photos; prohibit misleading images and counterfeit or restricted listings.
  • Offer report/block tools and clear community rules.
  • Encourage public meetups and item inspection; users should avoid suspicious advance-payment requests.
  • Show promoted listings transparently and never sell fake reviews or rankings.
